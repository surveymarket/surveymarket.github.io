How it works

From question to decision in four steps

  1. Write your questions

    Start from one of our templates or write your own. We check every question for wording that could bias the answers.

  2. Choose who answers

    Your own customers through a link, or people from a target group you describe: city, age range, interests.

  3. We collect the responses

    Most surveys reach their target number of responses in three to seven days. You can watch progress live.

  4. Get your report

    A short report with charts and the three things the answers say most clearly, plus the raw data if you want it.
